THE NORTHLINE STORY

Old-school welcome.
New-school standards.

We built Northline for the neighbourhood — a calm, considered place where the cut is excellent, the welcome is genuine, and nobody is checking the clock. Our barbers bring years of craft, but never take themselves too seriously.

Come in for the cut. Stay for the local recommendations, the record playing, or the debate about the best breakfast in Pretoria.
