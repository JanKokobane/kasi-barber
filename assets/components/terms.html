Terms & conditions

1. Bookings. When you book an appointment through our website, you are agreeing to arrive at the selected time. If you are running late, please call the shop — we can only hold your chair for 10 minutes before we need to offer it to the next person.

2. Cancellations and rescheduling. We understand plans change. Please give us at least 4 hours' notice if you need to cancel or reschedule. Repeated no-shows may be asked to prepay for future bookings.

3. Pricing. All prices are listed in South African Rand (R) and include VAT. Prices are subject to change, but any booked appointment will be honoured at the price shown at the time of booking.

4. First-visit discount. The R10 first-visit offer applies to new customers only and is limited to one per person. It cannot be combined with other offers.

5. Kids cuts. Our Kids Cut service is for children under 12. A parent or guardian must remain on the premises during the appointment.

6. Conduct. We ask all customers to be respectful of the shop, our barbers, and other customers. We reserve the right to decline service in cases of abusive or disruptive behaviour.

7. Payment. We accept cash and card payments at the shop. Online prepayment is not currently required unless stated otherwise at booking.

8. Changes to these terms. We may update these terms from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.

If you have a question about these terms, email hello@northline.barber or call the shop during opening hours.
